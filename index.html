The Problem

India's $130B Events Industry
Runs on WhatsApp & Word-of-Mouth

1.2 Crore weddings happen in India every year. 85–90% of planning is completely manual, and incumbents suffer 85%–92% booking leakage. That's the gap UTSAV fills.

😤

2x–3x "Wedding Tax" & Opacity

Vendors inflate quotes by 200%–300% for weddings vs identical corporate/private events. Pricing is opaque and unpredictable. 78% of couples feel overcharged, while 85%–90% of coordination is done manually through chaotic WhatsApp groups and calls.

📊 78% feel overcharged · 85%+ manual WhatsApp
Source: WedMeGood 5th Annual Report, IJRTI Research
🗺️

85%–92% Discovery-to-Booking Leakage

75% of couples discover vendors online, but <10% contract online. Classified portals broadcast 1 lead to 5–10 competing vendors, yielding dismal 1%–5% conversion rates. Platforms monetize raw leads, not completed bookings, causing massive leakage.

📱 85%–92% drop-off before contract · 1%–5% lead conversion
Source: IMARC Group 2025, WeddingPro & Weddingkart Benchmarks
💸

Cash-Flow Traps & Advance Forfeiture

Average wedding spend is ₹12.5L nationally (20% of family lifetime wealth) and ₹39.5L in metros. Vendors suffer 15%–30% delayed milestone receivables (30–90 days late), while couples risk 100% advance forfeiture with zero standardized SLAs.

💳 ₹12.5L national / ₹39.5L urban avg spend — Jefferies
Source: Jefferies 2024, WedMeGood 2025, Recordent MSME Data
Why Now

4 Converging Tailwinds Make 2026 the Year

The market conditions that enable UTSAV to win are aligning right now — not 5 years ago, not 3 years from now.

01

UPI + RBI Payment Aggregator Escrow Rails

RBI's consolidated 2024–2025 Payment Aggregator guidelines enable compliant multi-vendor milestone split escrows (Razorpay/Cashfree) without touching marketplace balance sheets.

✓ Milestone trust infrastructure ready
02

85.4% Couples Self-Planning (DIY Boom)

85.4% of modern Indian couples now plan weddings independently without full-time wedding planners (WedMeGood). The mobile-first generation demands an operational OS, not an ad directory.

✓ Mobile-first generation is the buyer
03

Favorable 2024 Tax Reforms

Finance Act 2024 slashed e-commerce TDS (Section 194-O) from 1.0% to 0.1%, and GST Council lowered TCS (Section 52) to 0.5% — creating the lowest tax friction for digital marketplaces in Indian history.

✓ Lowest digital compliance friction
