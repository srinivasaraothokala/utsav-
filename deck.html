The Industry Dilemma

High-Ticket, High-Emotion, Zero Structural Trust.

85%–90% of vendor supply operates unorganized. The lack of standard contracts, verified pricing, and escrow rails creates acute friction on both sides.

Consumer & Couple Pain Points
The Chaos of Manual Planning

  • 10–15 Vendor Coordination Matrix: 85.4% of couples plan independently without an agency, coordinating via chaotic WhatsApp groups.

  • Working Capital Squeeze: Vendors incur upfront staff, decor, and floral costs before final client settlement.

The Macro Inflection

Why Now? Fintech Rails, Tax Reforms & Gen-Z.

Three structural shifts have converged to make full-stack wedding formalization legally and technologically viable in 2024–2026.

Catalyst #1 · RBI Compliance
Payment Aggregator Split Escrow

Under RBI PA guidelines, marketplaces cannot hold funds in corporate accounts. Authorized Payment Aggregators (Razorpay / Cashfree) now provide compliant Split Nodal Escrow APIs. This allows automatic, programmatic milestone disbursement (T+1) while deducting platform commission legally.

Catalyst #2 · Tax Modernization
TDS & TCS Compliance Relief

Under the Finance Act 2024, Section 194-O TDS for e-commerce operators was slashed from 1.0% down to 0.1% (effective Oct 2024), and GST TCS was cut to 0.5%. This removed the tax penalty that historically drove small vendors to prefer cash over digital records.

Catalyst #3 · Digital Shift
UPI Normalization & Gen-Z Hosts

38% of wedding payments are now settled digitally (WedMeGood 2025). 87% of modern couples conduct vendor research on visual platforms, demanding digital contracts, online booking transparency, and flexible financing over traditional cash transactions.
